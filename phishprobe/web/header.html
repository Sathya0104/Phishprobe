Header Analyzer

1 How do I copy the header?

Gmail
  1. Open the email and click ⋮ (More) → Show original.
  2. Press Ctrl+A then Ctrl+C to copy the whole header.
  3. Want the full .eml? Click Download original and open the file in a text editor.
Yahoo Mail
  1. Open the email and click ⋮ (More) → View raw message.
  2. Select everything (Ctrl+A) and copy (Ctrl+C).
  3. This gives you the full source (header + body) in one go.
Outlook (web & desktop)
  1. Open the email and click ⋮ → View → View message source.
  2. Copy the text that opens.
  3. Desktop app: open the email → File → Properties → copy Internet headers.

Only the header part is needed here. For the complete analysis (body, links, attachments) use the Full Email Analyzer.

2 Paste the email header

Paste the header (or upload a file) and click Analyze. Analysis may take up to 2 minutes while reputation lookups complete.
