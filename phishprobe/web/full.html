1 How do I copy the raw email?

Gmail
  1. Open the email and click ⋮ (More) → Show original.
  2. Click Download original to get the .eml file.
  3. Open the .eml in a text editor and copy everything.
Yahoo Mail
  1. Open the email and click ⋮ (More) → View raw message.
  2. Select everything (Ctrl+A) and copy (Ctrl+C).
  3. That is the complete source - header and body together.
Outlook (web & desktop)
  1. Open the email and click ⋮ → View → View message source.
  2. Copy the full text that opens.
  3. Desktop app: open the email → File → Properties → copy Internet headers.

Paste everything (headers and body), or upload the .eml file directly.

2 Paste the complete raw email

Paste the complete raw email and click Analyze. Analysis usually completes in under a minute while reputation lookups run.
